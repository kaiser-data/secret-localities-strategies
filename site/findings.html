Audit design

What auditors should change

Record the input shape

Preserve literal system content, template path, role sequence, and turn count. “No custom system prompt” is not evidence that the model received no system text.

Separate two questions

Score confessional behavior separately from candidate-specific principal identification. One can repeat without establishing the other.

Use hostile controls

Include fictional and nonsense candidates, reversals, neutral phrasing, and false-premise probes—not only plausible real-world entities.

Match controls

Compare A and B against C and base under identical prompts, frames, seeds, and decoding.

Report intervals

Show seed spread, uncertainty intervals, exclusions, and calibrated detector power instead of a single pass/fail percentage.

Keep raw traces

Seal prompts and plans before inference, then retain responses, rendering metadata, seeds, revisions, and evaluation provenance for re-analysis.
