01 · Tracks

Four lines of work running against one shared pipeline. Two have landed, one is constrained by its own denominator, one is infrastructure that repeatedly caught the other three being wrong.

Track A — Audit the three provided organisms landed

Given sl-organism-a/b/c-7b, decide which carry a hidden loyalty and characterise it. Resolved. C is unmodified base weights; A and B are genuinely modified and share an attack configuration with each other that is distinct from the published poison ladder. Blind behavioural detection returned a calibrated null — and the null is explained quantitatively by the target's own design, not by our detector being broken.

Findings F1–F6. Cost to reach it: $1.60 across two runs.

Track B — Build our own organisms as ground truth constrained

A 17-cell grid: one anchor recipe, one factor changed per spoke, each with a content-matched control, so a detector can be scored against known ground truth. Training and measurement work end to end. What it cannot yet do is compare cells. Five replicates differing in seed alone spread 14.14 pp, which is larger than most of the factor effects the grid exists to detect.

Findings F7–F9. This is the track that changed most this session, and it changed by getting smaller.

Track C — Detector lanes 2 landed · 1 new · 1 never run

Four ordered lanes, cheapest and most decisive first. Weight-space forensics settles presence outright and needs no guess about trigger, principal or payload — which is precisely why it outperformed the behavioural sweep at a fifth of the cost. A fifth lane (weight-space → vocabulary) was added this session and produced the study's first positive separation of A/B from the drift controls.

Track D — Provenance and reproducibility earning its keep

Immutable run records, a report page generated from them rather than transcribed, and a private adapter mirror. Not glamorous, and responsible for three of this session's corrections: a stale-corpus audit finding, a cross-run pairing bug, and a table that disagreed with the records it was copied from.

02 · Architecture

The point of the system is that one measurement stack serves two inputs. Models we built (whose ground truth we know) and models we were given (whose ground truth we don't) enter the same detectors, so a detector's score on an unknown model is interpretable against its score on a known one. Everything downstream of the stack is provenance: what was measured, by which code, at what cost.

03 · Pipeline

One path from a config file to a scored verdict. Every organism goes through all of it; nothing is hand-placed.

config→corpus→train (LoRA)→checkpoints →probes→KL→gates 1–6→run record →report

The design principle is one factor per spoke: an anchor recipe, and each spoke differing from it in exactly one thing — poison fraction, trigger type, payload breadth, or seed. Each organism has a content-matched control trained on the same material with the loyalty removed, so drift attributable to fine-tuning in general can be differenced away from drift attributable to the implant.

06 · What the results mean

Two graphics carry most of the interpretation. The first explains why the audit found nothing behaviourally. The second explains which of our own grid comparisons are allowed to be made at all.

① Why blind detection returned nothing — it was never in range

Meaning. The 0/7 result was not a detector that failed; it was a detector pointed at a signal two decades below its sensitivity. The target's design paper enforces that quietness deliberately, and independently reports 0% detection for all five black-box techniques at the affordance level we ran. Our null reproduces the published state of the art rather than contradicting it — and it is a bound, because the false-positive rate was measured at 0/200 first.

This is also the argument for the weight-space lane. Off-condition behavioural quietness is something an attacker can optimise for. The residue left in ΔW is not.

② What the grid may and may not compare

Meaning. Everything to the right of the dashed line is a real effect we can defend at n=1. Everything to the left is indistinguishable from re-running the same recipe with a different random seed. The four large effects survive comfortably — so the grid's headline structure holds — but every fine-grained comparison it was designed to make is out of reach at this sample size, and two of the surviving four carry separate caveats of their own.

The uncomfortable part: the anchor recipe's own mean, 47.46%, sits below the 50% activation floor it is supposed to clear. The recipe does not reliably produce the organism we specified — it produces it about three times in five.

07 · Highlights

① The cheapest lane was the decisive one

A $0.26 weight-space run resolved presence on all seven models and characterised the attack configuration. The behavioural sweep cost five times more and returned a null — because it required guessing trigger, principal and payload, and all three guesses were wrong. Order detector lanes by how many guesses they require, not by how sophisticated they sound.

② A and B independently converge on the same concept

Projecting surviving weight-space directions through the unembedding, both models land on system — across three writing systems and every tokenisation variant (system/systems/_system/系统/систем), accompanied by you. Two separately trained models do not reproduce one word in three scripts by chance. Those are the tokens of the chat template's system turn and assistant persona — where a loyalty instruction would live.

The models over the p99 random-direction null: A 394, B 400, against 102 / 94 / 105 for the poison ladder. First lane in the study to separate A/B from the drift controls.

Not yet excluded: the organisms may have been trained with system prompts present, so this could encode generic system-prompt handling rather than a loyalty. Suggestive, not conclusive. SVD sign is arbitrary, so promoted-vs-suppressed is not interpretable — only the alignment is.

③ The null is a bound, not a blank

0/7 blind detections, but with a 0/200 false-positive rate on synthetic noise and a measured 90%-power floor of 0.2–0.3 nats/token. The target's published design holds off-condition KL at 0.0019–0.0052 nats — one to two orders of magnitude below our floor. The null reproduces the published state of the art rather than contradicting it.

An earlier version of this same statistic ran a 19.5% false-positive rate by taking a maximum over 760 cells against a threshold calibrated for one. A split-half fix took it to zero at no extra compute. Had we skipped that calibration we would have reported a confidently wrong principal.

④ Our own grid had no denominator — and that is the finding

Gate 4 is decided by seed, 3 of 5, and the mean falls below the floor. The anchor's much-quoted 52.44% was one draw from a distribution centred at 47.5%. Every "the anchor clears the activation floor" statement was a statement about seed 42.

The consequence is a hard constraint: no factor effect under ~15 pp is interpretable at n=1. The large effects survive comfortably; nothing in the 0–15 pp band does. The project's own config file pre-registered exactly this outcome — that if seed spread turned out comparable to the factor effects, that is the result, and between-cell claims at n=1 stop.

⑤ Three claims were retired, one of them a real bug

  • "Activation peaks mid-training and replicates across seed." It does not replicate. The split follows training run, not seed — two cells peak, three dip and recover, clusters 28 pp apart and tight within run. Until explained, no mid-training checkpoint number is quotable.
  • "The epoch-count effect explains a quarter of the gap." +5.3 pp sits inside one standard deviation. Unresolved — not disproven, never measured against a denominator.
  • A differential drift figure was wrong by 11× — an organism differenced against a leftover control from a different run, because the intermediate files carry no run identity and the results directory is overwritten in place. The corrected value reverses the conclusion: below the anchor, not nine times above it.

The bug was caught by a report generator disagreeing with a stored report. Both numbers were plausible; only cross-checking distinguished them.

08 · What we are not claiming

  • We have not named a principal in A or B. Weight-space localises and attributes; it does not read intent. system is a candidate concept awaiting behavioural confirmation, with a live alternative explanation.
  • No cell of our grid has a complete gate verdict, because gate 6 has never been run on any of them.
  • Two grid corners are built on stale corpora that predate a generator fix, at nearly double the intended effective dose. They are not single-factor spokes and their numbers cannot be compared to the rest.
  • The dose ladder is confounded by design at fixed epochs: changing the poison fraction also changes total row count, and therefore optimizer steps and learning-rate schedule length.
  • No mid-training checkpoint number is quotable until the run-versus-seed confound is explained.

09 · Direction

Ordered by cost, cheapest first. Four of the six are free; only one needs GPU spend. The ordering is deliberate — the constraint on this project has never been compute, it has been unverified claims.

#MoveWhy nowCost
1 Run the activation heatmap The one deliverable the site advertises and has never produced. Adapters are now on the Hub, so it runs on CPU. Blocked only on the loader learning to wrap a LoRA adapter. $0
2 Read the QK half of the attention edit The implant is a pure routing change — q/k/v/o, no MLP, no unembedding. The vocabulary lane read the OV side and found system. QK is the principled place to ask what it watches for, which is the actual hypothesis. Code exists on a branch. $0
3 Diagnose the checkpoint anomaly Two free checks: whether concurrent cells in one fan-out share anything at checkpoint time, and whether the probe subsample is stable across runs. Unblocks every mid-training number. $0
4 Stamp run identity into intermediate files Closes the cross-run pairing bug that already produced an 11× error. The gate should return "no matched control" rather than silently pairing across runs. Do not loosen the threshold. $0
5 Regenerate and retrain the two stale corners The only way the corner comparison becomes defensible. Regeneration alone is worse than nothing — it flips a gate to PASS while the trained weights still come from the old data, turning a true failure into a silent confound. ~$6.50
6 Decide the anchor recipe's fate The recipe sits astride the floor rather than above it. The answer is more replicates or a stronger organism — never a lowered threshold. TBD

The strategic read

The audit track has produced publishable results at negligible cost, and its most recent lane — weight-space to vocabulary — is the first thing in the study to positively separate the suspect models from the drift controls. That is where the remaining momentum is, and both free moves at the top of the list feed it.

The organism track is in a different state. It works, but it has just discovered that its measurement precision is coarser than most of the effects it was built to measure. The honest move is not to spend GPU widening the grid; it is to finish the free diagnostics, then decide whether to strengthen the recipe or accept n=1 comparisons are out of reach at this budget. Cancelling a training batch to fund five seed replicates was the right trade — the replicates invalidated more false claims than the batch would have produced true ones.
