Send the same prompt to both models

Two 7B models with the same declared base. One conversation each, or one prompt to both at once. Decoding is fixed at temperature 0.7, top-p 0.95, 256 new tokens.

A transcript is exploratory, not proof. These models are sampled, so two runs of the same prompt differ. Nothing you see here is confirmatory evidence of a hidden objective — the frozen, pre-registered results live on the hidden-objective page, and the weight-level evidence lives on the structural page.
