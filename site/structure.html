Where the models were edited

Every cell is a real measured value from a weight diff against the declared base: rel_fro = ‖Wmodel − Wbase‖F / ‖Wbase‖F. Rows are transformer layers 0–27, columns are the four attention projections. This localises the modification and identifies its family. It cannot identify its purpose.

Accessible table — the same values, without colour
The A-versus-B view shows edit magnitude only: log10((rel_fro_A + 1e-12) / (rel_fro_B + 1e-12)). Positive means A was changed more at that site than B. It is not tensor direction, not an objective, and not loyalty. A and B share one fixed colour domain so intensity is comparable between them.
